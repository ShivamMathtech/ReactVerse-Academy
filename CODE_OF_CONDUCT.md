# Code of conduct

ReactVerse Academy welcomes learners and contributors regardless of experience, background, identity, or ability.

- Be patient with beginner questions and explain the reasoning behind corrections.
- Discuss code and ideas without attacking people.
- Respect different perspectives, accessibility needs, and communication styles.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, disclose personal information, or publish sexualized or hostile content.
- Credit other people's work and follow the project's license and contribution rules.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation proportionately. They should explain decisions where safe and consider context and opportunities to repair harm. These expectations apply to project spaces and representation of the project in public.

Report concerns privately to the repository owner through a private contact channel listed on their GitHub profile. This source archive does not invent a contact address. If a maintainer is involved, seek another available maintainer or use the hosting platform's reporting process. Do not publish private evidence in a public issue.
